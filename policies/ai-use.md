# Acceptable Use of AI

Aldermoor Group (fictional company). Owner: Chief Financial Officer and Head of Information Security.

## 1. Approved tools

Only AI assistants approved by Information Security may be used with company information. Public AI tools must not be given confidential financial data, customer data or personal data.

## 2. Checking outputs

People remain accountable for anything produced with AI. Figures, policy answers and financial statements produced with AI must be checked against the source before they are used or shared.

## 3. Personal data

AI assistants must not be used to look up or reveal information about an individual employee, such as their salary or performance.
