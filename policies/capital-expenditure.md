# Capital Expenditure Policy

Aldermoor Group (fictional company). Owner: Chief Financial Officer.

## 1. What is capitalised

An asset is capitalised when it costs more than £1,000 and will be used for more than one year. Items below this threshold are expensed.

## 2. Useful lives

2.1 Laptops and IT equipment are depreciated over 3 years on a straight-line basis.

2.2 Office furniture is depreciated over 5 years.

2.3 Capitalised software development is amortised over 3 years from the date it goes live.

## 3. Business cases

Capital projects over £50,000 need a business case with a payback analysis and must be approved by the investment committee.
