# Revenue Recognition Policy

Aldermoor Group (fictional company). Owner: Financial Controller.

## 1. Subscriptions

Subscription revenue is recognised evenly over the subscription term, month by month, starting when the customer gets access.

## 2. Set-up fees

One-off set-up fees are recognised over the expected customer life, currently 24 months, not when they are invoiced.

## 3. Discounts

Discounts are spread over the contract term in the same way as the revenue they relate to.

## 4. Refunds and credits

Expected refunds and service credits reduce revenue in the month the obligation arises, not when they are paid.
