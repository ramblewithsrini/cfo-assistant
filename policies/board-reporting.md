# Board Reporting Standard

Aldermoor Group (fictional company). Owner: Chief Financial Officer.

## 1. Board pack contents

The monthly board pack contains a KPI dashboard, the profit and loss account against budget and forecast, the 13-week cash flow forecast, and the top risks with their owners.

## 2. Timing

The board pack is sent to board members 5 working days before the board meeting.

## 3. Commentary

Commentary explains the three largest variances against budget in plain English, with the action being taken on each.
