# Corporate Card Policy

Aldermoor Group (fictional company). Owner: Financial Controller.

## 1. Limits

Corporate cards have a monthly limit of £5,000. Higher limits need approval from the Financial Controller.

## 2. Use

Corporate cards are for business expenses only. Personal use is not allowed, even if repaid.

## 3. Reconciliation

Cardholders must reconcile their statement and attach receipts within 10 days of the statement date. Cards are suspended if two statements in a row are not reconciled.
