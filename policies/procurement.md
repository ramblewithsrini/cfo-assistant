# Procurement Policy

Aldermoor Group (fictional company). Owner: Chief Financial Officer.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Aldermoor Group buys goods and services, who can approve a purchase, and how we choose suppliers.

## 2. Raising a purchase order

Every purchase over £500 needs a purchase order raised in the finance system before the order is placed with the supplier. Invoices without a matching purchase order are not paid until the budget holder confirms the spend in writing.

## 3. Approval limits

3.1 Purchases up to £2,500 are approved by the budget holder.

3.2 Purchases from £2,500 to £10,000 need approval from the budget holder and the finance business partner.

3.3 Purchases over £10,000 need approval from the Chief Financial Officer.

3.4 Purchases over £100,000 need three written quotes and approval from both the Chief Financial Officer and the Chief Executive.

## 4. Splitting purchases

A purchase must not be split into smaller orders to stay under an approval limit. Related orders to the same supplier within 30 days are treated as one purchase.

## 5. Choosing suppliers

New suppliers must pass the supplier onboarding checks in the Payments Policy before their first order. Preferred suppliers should be used wherever one exists for the category.
