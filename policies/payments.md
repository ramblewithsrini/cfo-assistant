# Payments Policy

Aldermoor Group (fictional company). Owner: Head of Treasury.

## 1. Payment runs

Supplier payment runs happen every Tuesday and Friday. Invoices approved by 12:00 on the day before a run are included in that run.

## 2. Approving payments

2.1 Every payment needs two people: one to prepare it and one to approve it.

2.2 Payments over £25,000 need a second approver from the treasury team.

## 3. Supplier bank details

3.1 A change to a supplier's bank details must be verified by calling the supplier on a phone number already held on file, never a number given in the request.

3.2 No payment is made to new bank details until the call-back is recorded in the finance system.

## 4. Urgent payments

Payments outside the normal runs need approval from the Head of Treasury and are made only for regulatory, payroll or legal deadlines.
