# Expenses Policy

Aldermoor Group (fictional company). Owner: Financial Controller.

## 1. Submitting claims

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed unless the Financial Controller agrees an exception.

## 2. Receipts

An itemised receipt is required for every expense over £25. For expenses of £25 or less, a description of the expense is enough.

## 3. Meals

3.1 Meals while travelling on business are reimbursed up to £30 a day in the UK and £45 a day outside the UK.

3.2 Alcohol is not reimbursed, except as part of client entertainment approved in advance by a director.

## 4. Mileage

Business mileage in a personal car is reimbursed at 45p a mile for the first 10,000 miles in a tax year and 25p a mile after that. Commuting between home and your usual office is not business mileage.

## 5. Approval

Claims are approved by the employee's line manager. Claims by directors are approved by the Chief Financial Officer.
