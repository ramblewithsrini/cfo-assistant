# Travel Policy

Aldermoor Group (fictional company). Owner: Financial Controller.

## 1. Booking

All business travel is booked through the TravelDesk at least 14 days before departure where possible. Bookings made outside the TravelDesk are not reimbursed without Financial Controller approval.

## 2. Flights

2.1 Flights under 6 hours are booked in economy.

2.2 Flights of 6 hours or more may be booked in business class with a director's approval.

## 3. Hotels

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to £180 a night in London and £130 a night elsewhere in the UK. Outside the UK, the TravelDesk rate for the city applies.

## 4. Rail

Rail travel is in standard class. First class is allowed only for journeys over 3 hours when booked at least 7 days ahead and cheaper than a flexible standard fare.
