# Forecasting and Variance Policy

Aldermoor Group (fictional company). Owner: Head of FP&A.

## 1. Rolling forecast

The 18-month rolling forecast is updated every quarter, in the second month of the quarter.

## 2. Variance commentary

Budget holders must explain any variance where actual results differ from forecast by more than 5% and by more than £20,000.

## 3. Cash forecast

Treasury maintains a 13-week cash flow forecast, updated every Monday.
