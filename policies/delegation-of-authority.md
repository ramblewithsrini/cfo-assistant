# Delegation of Authority

Aldermoor Group (fictional company). Owner: Company Secretary.

## 1. Signing contracts

1.1 Contracts with a total value up to £50,000 may be signed by a director.

1.2 Contracts up to £250,000 must be signed by the Chief Financial Officer.

1.3 Contracts over £250,000 need board approval before signature.

## 2. Hiring

New permanent roles not in the approved budget need approval from the Chief Executive and the Chief Financial Officer.

## 3. Payroll changes

Changes to pay or bonus need approval from the HR Director and the finance business partner for the area.
