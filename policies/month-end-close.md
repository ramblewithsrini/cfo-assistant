# Month-End Close Procedure

Aldermoor Group (fictional company). Owner: Financial Controller.

## 1. Timetable

The close follows working days (WD) after the month end.

1.1 WD1, 12:00: cut-off for accruals and prepayments.

1.2 WD3: intercompany balances agreed and reconciled.

1.3 WD5: management accounts sent to the Chief Financial Officer.

1.4 WD7: board pack finalised.

## 2. Accruals

An accrual is required for any goods or services received before month end without an invoice, where the value is over £5,000.

## 3. Balance sheet reconciliations

All balance sheet accounts are reconciled every month. Reconciling items older than 60 days are escalated to the Financial Controller.
